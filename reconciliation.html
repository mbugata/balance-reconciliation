Approach & assumptions (for reviewers)
  • Matching logic: for each bank-statement date, expected balance = opening balance + sum of every transaction dated on or before that date.
  • Same-day transactions are summed together — the bank only gives one balance per day, so we can't verify ordering within a day.
  • "New break" vs "carried forward": we separately compare each day's bank balance change to that day's transaction total. If they agree, any cumulative diff is inherited from an earlier day (carried forward, shown amber). If they disagree, today is where the mismatch originated (shown red).
  • Tolerance: differences under the configured threshold (default 1¢) are treated as floating-point noise, not real discrepancies.
  • Unmatched transaction dates (transactions dated after the bank statement ends, or on a date with no corresponding entry) are called out separately since they can't be reconciled yet — see warning box above the chart, when applicable.
